# The Living Hearth (Digital Sanctuary)
> *“A safe, non-solicitation, learning-first social platform focused exclusively on religion, spirituality, and personal faith practice.”*

---

## 1. Product Vision & Ethical Foundation
The Living Hearth is an age-inclusive digital sanctuary designed to reject the extractive, dopamine-driven conventions of modern social media. 

### Core Principles
- **Safety, Consent & Educational Integrity First:** User emotional well-being and privacy rank unconditionally above engagement metrics, viral loops, and ad impressions.
- **Strict Non-Solicitation:** Zero fundraising campaigns, zero recruitment or multi-level schemes, zero commercial pitches, and zero unsolicited proselytizing direct messages.
- **Not a Dating or Matching Service:** Completely devoid of romantic or dating mechanics.
- **Private By Default:** No public directories indexing real-world location, legal names, or spiritual search histories.
- **Federal & HIPAA Compliance Safeguards:** Built-in Protected Health Information (PHI) shield preventing accidental disclosure of clinical diagnoses, 100% zero-tracker policy, and direct 988 Crisis Lifeline fast access.

## 2. Sacred Geometry Design System
Built on mathematical proportions that transcend any single creed:
1. **Golden Ratio ($\phi \approx 1.618$):** Layout column splits (61.8% primary content / 38.2% reflection rhythm), card aspect ratios, and typography scale steps.
2. **Fibonacci Rhythm:** Spacing multiples ($8\text{px}, 13\text{px}, 21\text{px}, 34\text{px}, 55\text{px}, 89\text{px}$) governing layout rhythm.
3. **Concentric Rings:** Used for room activity pulses, prayer confirmation ripples, and progress tracking.
4. **Vesica Piscis:** Dual-circle intersection used as the structural guide for side-by-side interfaith comparative study and mutual consent confirmations.
5. **Breath Motion:** $400\text{--}700\text{ms}$ ease-in-out breathing curves; full reduced-motion override.

## 5. Accessibility Compliance (WCAG 2.2 Level AA / AAA)
- **High Contrast Mode:** Switches to high-contrast palette meeting $\ge 7:1$ AAA ratio.
- **Dynamic Text Scaling:** Responsive from $100\%$ to $200\%$ without horizontal scroll or truncated text.
- **Reduced Motion Support:** Respects system `prefers-reduced-motion` and provides an in-app toggle replacing breath animations with calm static states.
- **Focus Rings:** Luminous $2\text{px}$ ring with $3\text{px}$ offset and shadow meeting WCAG 2.4.11 visibility standards.
- **Touch Targets:** All interactive controls maintain $\ge 44 \times 44\text{px}$ touch targets.
