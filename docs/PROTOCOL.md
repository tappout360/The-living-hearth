## Preamble: The Sovereign Sanctuary Charter

The Living Hearth (FaithConnect) is designed and operated as an uncompromised digital sanctuary focused exclusively on religion, spirituality, and personal faith practice. It rejects the extractive mechanics of modern social media:

1. **Safety Above Growth:** Client safety, emotional well-being, affirmative consent, and educational integrity rank strictly above user acquisition, engagement time, and viral reach.
2. **Absolute Non-Solicitation:** The platform is not a marketplace, not a fundraising portal, not a dating service, and not an arena for religious recruitment or conversion.
3. **Maximal Inclusivity ("Leave Out None"):** Every human spiritual tradition—indigenous, ancient, world historical, modern, esoteric, and contemplative—is welcomed with equal academic rigor and profound dignity.

## 1. HIPAA & Federal Healthcare Privacy Protocol
*Compliance Reference: 45 CFR Part 160 & Part 164 (HIPAA Privacy & Security Rules)*

### 1.1 Context & Vulnerability Recognition
Spiritual practice and intentional prayer frequently occur at moments of profound human vulnerability: acute medical crises, terminal diagnoses, bereavement, substance recovery, and psychological distress. While The Living Hearth is a non-clinical spiritual sanctuary, it voluntarily upholds clinical-grade privacy safeguards to protect user health disclosures from commercial exploitation.

### 1.2 Real-Time In-Browser Protected Health Information (PHI) Shield
All text entered into the Prayer Sanctuary composer, room dialogue boxes, and invitation notes is screened by a client-side real-time scanner before transmission:
* **Detection Categories:** Diagnostic terms (e.g., *chemotherapy, metastatic, biopsy, stage 4*), clinical departments (*ICU, oncology, psychiatric inpatient*), medications, and medical record identifiers.
* **Non-Punitive Client Warning:** When detected, the user receives an immediate educational prompt:
  > *"Notice (HIPAA & Health Privacy): We detected clinical terms or health identifiers. To safeguard personal medical privacy under federal standards, we suggest keeping specific medical details in your Private Journal or using gentle generalized phrasing (e.g., 'healing and restoration for a loved one undergoing treatment')."*
* **Safe Harbor De-identification:** Practitioners are actively guided to maintain spiritual intentionality without cataloguing sensitive clinical histories.

### 1.3 Zero-Tracker & Anti-Surveillance Architecture
* **Strict Prohibition on Commercial Ad Pixels:** Under federal guidance regarding third-party tracking technologies (HHS OCR Bulletin Dec 2022 / March 2024), **zero** advertising SDKs, Meta Pixels, Google Analytics trackers, or data broker scripts are embedded in the application code.
* **Content Security Policy (CSP):** Enforced via `vercel.json` with strict `default-src 'self'`, forbidding unauthorized cross-origin data leakage.
* **Network Encryption:** TLS 1.3 enforced on all transit; HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) with 1-year preload header.

### 1.4 Client Data Sovereignty: Export & Cryptographic Purge
In compliance with federal data portability and privacy sovereignty:
* **One-Click Vault Export:** Practitioners can export their complete sanctuary archive (private journals, prayers, learning progress) in machine-readable JSON format at any time.
* **Immediate Cryptographic Purge:** Practitioners can execute a one-click local and remote wipe, instantly deleting all private reflections without retention.

## 2. Non-Solicitation & Zero-Commercialization Protocol

### 2.1 The Non-Solicitation Covenant
Religious exploitation frequently manifests through financial solicitation, tithing pressure, multi-level schemes, and commercialized spiritual products. The Living Hearth enforces a zero-tolerance policy against all commercial extraction:
* **Prohibited Elements:**
  * Donation URLs (*Venmo, CashApp, PayPal, GoFundMe, Patreon*).
  * Cryptocurrency wallet addresses and token promotions.
  * Commercial product pitching, coaching services, and paid spiritual courses.
  * Multi-level marketing (MLM) and recruitment funnels.
  * Proselytizing for monetary dues or mandatory organizational membership.

### 2.2 Automated Scanner & Interception
Any message containing financial keywords or transaction links is automatically blocked at the client level before dispatch, displaying the platform's non-solicitation pledge.

### 2.3 Escalation Matrix
1. **First Incident:** Automated block + private educational notice explaining the sanctuary's non-commercial covenant.
2. **Second Incident:** Temporary 7-day restriction from posting in public circles.
3. **Third Incident / Wilful Bad Faith:** Permanent suspension from public circles and revocation of community privileges.

## 3. Affirmative Consent & Safe Direct Communication Protocol

### 3.1 Mutual Affirmative Consent Mandate
Direct prayer requests, personal intention sharing, and companion connection requests require **two-way explicit consent**:
* **No Cold Solicitations:** Users cannot send unsolicited private intentions to strangers. A prior connection or explicit affirmative check-box agreement is mandatory.
* **No Email or Phone Harvesting:** User discovery occurs exclusively via self-chosen sanctuary display names. Contact books, phone numbers, and external email addresses are never requested or accessed.

### 3.2 Privacy-Preserving Silent Rejection
To prevent retaliatory harassment, emotional manipulation, or guilt:
* When a user **declines** an invitation or direct prayer request, the invitation is quietly removed from their queue.
* **Zero notification or rejection reason** is sent to the prospective sender. The sender's interface displays only that the request is no longer pending.

### 3.3 Universal Mute & Block Sovereignty
* Practitioners may mute or block any companion or circle with a single tap.
* When a blocked user attempts to send an intention, the system fails silently with a calm generic message (*"This companion is currently not receiving direct intentions"*), preserving the blocker’s privacy and safety.

## 4. Crisis Intervention & Mental Health Safety Protocol (988 Lifeline)

### 4.1 Non-Clinical Boundary Disclaimer
The Living Hearth is a contemplative sanctuary for prayer, meditation, and educational study. It is **not** a healthcare provider, mental health clinic, or suicide hotline.

### 4.2 Immediate 988 Lifeline Integration
* **Persistent Crisis Anchor:** A prominent, high-contrast fast-link to the **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** is permanently integrated into the header, profile, and emergency resource footers.
* **Direct Emergency Dialing:** Tapping the 988 button immediately initiates a secure call or text to `988` (free, confidential, 24/7 care across the United States and Canada).
* **Compassionate Non-Punitive Escalation:** If severe distress or self-harm keywords are detected, the user is gently presented with emergency care options rather than banned or locked out.

## 6. Room Moderation & Circle Governance Protocol

### 6.1 Role Hierarchies & Responsibilities
* **Circle Elders:** Recognized lineage scholars, teachers, and ordained ministers who hold space for deep theological nuance and pastoral reassurance.
* **Circle Guides:** Trained community facilitators responsible for maintaining calm atmosphere, de-escalating philosophical disputes, and managing queue reports.
* **Participants:** Verified practitioners who have affirmed the circle's non-solicitation and respect covenants.

### 6.2 Active Circle Thresholds
* **Reflection Pause (Slow Mode):** Rooms enforce a 30-to-120 second reflection interval between successive messages to eliminate impulsive reactive debates and foster contemplative slowness.
* **Affirmative Covenant Confirmation:** Entry into any moderated room requires one-tap affirmation of the room's specific guidelines.
* **Atmospheric Audio & Visual Sync:** Rooms dynamically load atmospheric layers (Learning, Practice, Discussion) to foster psychological attunement.

### 6.3 Restorative Reporting & Triage Queue
* Participants can flag any message with one tap.
* Flagged messages enter the **Circle Reporting Queue** with specific categorizations (*solicitation, proselytizing, clinical PHI, harassment*).
* Circle Guides can apply restorative remedies:
  * *Dismiss Report:* False positives or harmless misunderstandings.
  * *Gentle Private Reflection Notice:* Reminds author of circle covenants without public shaming.
  * *Mute from Circle:* Prevents bad-faith disruption while keeping read-only access to learning paths intact.

## 7. Cross-Platform Technical Security & Accessibility Protocol

### 7.1 Cross-Platform Parity
All core sanctuary flows (Dashboard, Pray, Learn, Rooms, Invites, Safety Vault) maintain identical feature parity across:
* **Desktop / Web Browser:** Reading max-width 720–800px; persistent side navigation; keyboard shortcuts (`P` for Pray, `Escape` to close).
* **Tablet (iPadOS / Android):** Adaptive multi-column grid; touch, stylus, and keyboard input.
* **Mobile & PWA:** Single-column layout; bottom navigation bar; standalone display mode via `public/manifest.json`.

### 7.2 Offline Resilience Protocol
* Private prayer journals and core reading paths are persisted locally in client storage.
* Intentions composed offline are queued in an encrypted local vault with a visual badge (`Saved offline (will sync)`) and sync seamlessly when network connectivity resumes.

### 7.3 Accessibility Protocol (WCAG 2.2 Level AA / AAA)
* **Font Scaling:** Dynamic typography scaling from 100% to 200% without clipping or layout collapse.
* **High Contrast Mode:** Pure black-and-white high-contrast mode for visual impairments.
* **Reduced Motion & Sensory Load:** Respects system `prefers-reduced-motion`; halts animated breath pulses and ducks ambient soundscapes.
* **Bidirectional Layouts:** True right-to-left (`dir="rtl"`) layout for Arabic (`ar`) and Hebrew (`he`).
* **Zero Auto-Play Ambient Sound:** Web Audio API harmonic drones require explicit user consent toggle before any sound is generated.
